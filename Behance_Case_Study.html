UX CASE STUDY · CAMPUS HOSTEL DESIGN

VUFIX — Hostel Maintenance App

Redefining the fragmented university living experience with a centralized, real-time SPA that bridges the communication gap between students and hostel administration.

Designer: Aryaman Saboo
Course: Human Computer Interaction (STET301)
Institution: Vijaybhoomi University
Timeline: Aug - Sep 2026
01 · CONTEXT & PROBLEM

The Communication Vacuum

Traditional maintenance reporting relies on physical ledgers, scattered emails, and broken feedback loops, leaving students frustrated and wardens overwhelmed.

Zero Visibility

Students report issues but have no way of knowing if a technician has been assigned, leading to repeated inquiries and anxiety.

Warden Overload

Administrators lack a centralized dashboard to track, prioritize, and assign hundreds of complaints efficiently across different hostel blocks.

Complex IA

Existing university portals require too many clicks to perform simple actions like logging a broken chair or a leaking tap.

02 · USER RESEARCH & EMPATHY

Mapping the Hostel Ecosystem

By mapping the dual-sided nature of hostel maintenance, we identified the exact friction points for both the complaining student and the resolving warden.

Persona 1: The Frustrated Student

Goal: Wants to report a broken AC and know exactly when it will be fixed without leaving their room.

  • Pain Point: Never knows the status of complaints.
  • Behavior: Often forgets to follow up until the issue becomes unbearable.
  • Need: A 1-click tracking timeline.

Persona 2: The Overwhelmed Warden

Goal: Needs to dispatch limited technical staff (plumbers, electricians) to the most urgent issues quickly.

  • Pain Point: Duplicate complaints and messy ledgers.
  • Behavior: Uses WhatsApp groups to coordinate with staff.
  • Need: A triage dashboard with simple assignment tools.
03 · INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Dual-Portal Synchronized State

VUFIX splits the architecture strictly by role, sharing a common reactive state layer to ensure instant bidirectional updates.

Student Portal Structure

1. Quick Auth / Fretbox Login

2. Dashboard (Active Stats)

3. 3-Step Guided Wizard (Report)

4. Live Stepper Timeline (Track)

Warden Portal Structure

1. Secure Admin Login

2. Triage Dashboard (Urgent Escalations)

3. Filterable Ticket List

4. Assignment Modal (Staff & ETA)

05 · USABILITY TESTING

Stress-Testing the Prototype

Tested with 5 real campus stakeholders (3 students, 2 wardens) using task-based scenarios to uncover friction points.

Identified Friction Point User Behavior Observed Heuristic Violation / Fix
Category Taxonomy Users hesitated unsure if "Furniture" included window blinds. Match System & Real World: Added sub-labels and clear icons to guide choice.
Escalation Visibility Missed the "Escalate" button on delayed tickets because it blended in. Aesthetic & Minimalist Design: Changed button to High-Vis Coral for urgency.
Submit Feedback Loop Repeatedly tapped "Submit" causing anxiety about duplicate tickets. Visibility of System Status: Implemented a 1.5s loading spinner state before success toast.
06 · PEER BENCHMARKING

Evaluating Against Classmates

We benchmarked VUFIX against three other classmate projects handling similar campus complaints to justify our architectural decisions.

Peer A (Chatbot UI)

Their Model: Conversational UI for reporting issues.

UX Takeaway: Fun, but too slow for urgent requests (e.g. bursting pipe). Validated VUFIX's rapid 3-step wizard.

Peer B (History First)

Their Model: Dashboard defaults to history, hiding the "New Complaint" button.

UX Takeaway: Increased interaction cost for the primary user goal. Validated VUFIX's prominent top-level Action button.
