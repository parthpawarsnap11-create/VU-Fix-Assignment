Problem Discovery

The "Black Hole" of Dormitory Maintenance

Paper logbooks and fragmented WhatsApp groups caused systemic failure across 4 residential blocks.

Zero Status Transparency
Students file physical complaints in hostel registers with no tracking, leading to endless anxiety and repeated visits to the warden office.
Misrouted Technician Triage
Wardens lacked structured categorization, routinely dispatching electrical technicians to plumbing emergencies, creating 48hr+ resolution lags.
Phantom Resolutions
Staff marked tickets "Done" on paper clipboards without resident verification, forcing students to re-register issues when repairs failed.

Information Architecture

Dual-Role Synchronized System Architecture

Purpose-built interfaces for both residential Students and the Hostel Warden Administration.

Student Portal (Aryaman Saboo)

  • Pre-Authenticated Identity: Automatically binds Hostel A, Block B3, Room B3-304.
  • Guided 3-Step Wizard: Category tiles ➔ Issue details ➔ Confirmation.
  • Live Stepper: Transparent milestone progress with assigned technician & ETA.
  • Two-Way Verification: Explicit "Yes, Fixed" vs "No, Still Broken" loop.

Warden Portal (Warden Rinu Babu)

  • High-Density Triage Dashboard: 5 real-time KPI stat cards with 1-click filters.
  • Urgent Escalation Banner: High-priority visual alert for delayed issues.
  • Specialist Staff Dispatch: Auto-matching trade roster (Plumbing, Electrical, IT).
  • Live Student Timeline Sync: Push internal warden notes directly to student app.

Usability Testing (25% Weightage)

Usability Testing & Broken Nielsen Heuristics

Evaluated across 5 real campus users (Aryaman, Ananya, Kabir, Priya, and Warden Asst. Dilip Rao).

Heuristic #2: Match System & Real World
Friction: Users hesitated between "Electrical" vs "Furniture" when reporting desk lamp socket repairs.
Fix: Enriched categories with contextual sub-labels (Electrical: Fan, Light, Socket, Geyser).
Heuristic #5: Error Prevention
Friction: Freshmen clicked "Escalate to Warden" 10 minutes after submitting, assuming it was a fast-track button.
Fix: Implemented dynamic SLA timer gating ("Eligible in 18 hrs") and mandatory reason prompt.
Heuristic #6: Recognition Rather Than Recall
Friction: Warden assistant struggled to remember staff specialties from plain employee names.
Fix: Added trade specialty badges & auto-recommendation matching in staff dispatch dropdown.
Heuristic #3: User Control & Freedom
Friction: Students hesitated to mark "No, Still Broken" fearing loss of prior repair records.
Fix: Added explicit modal assuring students that reopening keeps full timeline history intact.
Usability Testing Across 5 Real Users & 10 Nielsen Heuristics Slide 4 / 7 · Pitch Time: 2:00 – 2:50 (50s)
System Robustness

System Architecture vs Unpredictable Human Behavior

Stress-testing the reactive state layer against erratic human interaction patterns.

Rapid Double-Clicking
Behavior: Users rapidly mashed submit button on slow connections.
Fix: State-level debouncing and submission lock flag preventing duplicate database writes.
Multi-Tab Desync
Behavior: Student escalated on Tab 1 while Warden resolved on Tab 2.
Fix: Window `storage` event broadcast listeners refreshing in-memory state automatically.
Malicious Input Payloads
Behavior: Users pasted raw HTML/scripts into issue descriptions.
Fix: Strict textContent node rendering and CSS break-word bounds preventing DOM injection.
