01 / Problem Discovery

The "Black Hole" of Dormitory Maintenance

In residential universities, maintenance breakdown is constant. However, paper logbooks and fragmented WhatsApp groups created systemic failure across 4 hostel blocks.

Zero Status Transparency

Students wrote complaints in paper registers at security desks. Once signed, tickets vanished into a void with no tracking or updates, leaving students in chronic anxiety.

Misrouted Technician Triage

Hostel wardens lacked structured categorization, routinely dispatching electrical technicians to plumbing emergencies, creating 48-hour resolution lags.

Phantom Resolutions

Staff marked tickets as "Done" on clipboards without resident verification. Students returned from class only to find water still leaking.

03 / Usability Testing (25% Weightage)

Stress-Testing with 5 Real Users & 10 Nielsen Heuristics

Concurrent Think-Aloud Protocol with 5 campus stakeholders: Aryaman Saboo, Ananya Sharma, Kabir Mehta, Priya Iyer, and Warden Assistant Mr. Dilip Rao.

Broken Heuristic #2: Match System & Real World

Friction: Electrical vs Furniture Dilemma

Users reporting a broken study desk lamp socket hesitated for 25+ seconds between Electrical and Furniture.

Design Fix: Enriched category cards with explicit micro-labels (Electrical: Fan, Light, Socket, Geyser; Furniture: Desk, Chair, Lock).
Broken Heuristic #5: Error Prevention

Friction: Premature Warden Escalation

Freshman users clicked "Escalate to Warden" 10 minutes after filing, mistakenly thinking it was a fast-track button.

Design Fix: Implemented dynamic SLA timer gating ("Eligible for Escalation in 18h") and a modal requiring a stated reason.
Broken Heuristic #6: Recognition Rather Than Recall

Friction: Staff Dispatch Trade Confusion

The warden assistant struggled to remember which technician handled specific repairs based solely on staff names in the dropdown.

Design Fix: Added verified trade specialty badges (Plumbing, Electrical, IT) and automated skill-matching presets.
Broken Heuristic #3: User Control and Freedom

Friction: Reopening Anxiety

Students hesitated to click "No, Still Broken" due to fear that it would delete their prior ticket or anger the maintenance staff.

Design Fix: Reassuring confirmation modal explaining that reopening keeps the complete history and triggers high-priority review.
