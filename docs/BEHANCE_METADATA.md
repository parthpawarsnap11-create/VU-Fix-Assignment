### 4. Project Description Summary (For Behance Preview Cards & Search Indexing)
> *"VUFIX is an end-to-end human-centered maintenance application engineered for residential university dormitories. Designed to bridge 1,200+ students with campus administration, VUFIX replaces opaque paper registers with a guided 3-step wizard, live progress stepper, student-driven warden escalation engine, and bidirectional resolution verification. Tested with 5 campus stakeholders and benchmarked across 3 student teams, VUFIX achieved an 89.0 SUS Score (97th percentile) and 96% task completion rate."*
